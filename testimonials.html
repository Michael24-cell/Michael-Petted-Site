JEFF GODSIL

Actor

"Michael Petted was just the coach I needed when I first got the crazy notion to become an actor. It was late in life when I arrived at that decision. I had been a writer, and had always been movie-crazy. But for some still inexplicable reason, I had never really been compelled to try acting. Once I got the bug though, I got it bad. I soon realized that it had chosen me. Not the other way around. I had merely been standing in my own way, intellectualizing away my basic desires and instincts. Once I made that leap, I was now faced with all the questions and insecurities that a new actor faces. After one brief stint with a rather lackluster class, I discovered the Beverly Hills Playhouse and met Michael. I was immediately taken with Michael, and the approach that he had adopted from his mentor, Milton Katselas. The emphasis was on scene study. Get up there and do it! Michael had told me on our first meeting that my life experience would help me on my journey as an actor. It was the first of many nods and nudges that were to help me to grow. For if there is any one benefit that I received from Michael's guidance over any other, it is in the growth of my own self-confidence. Michael's classes always provide the space to grow, to take chances, to discover. His love of the craft and his genuine desire for your success make those few hours the highlight of the week. And with Michael you discover that you never stop learning …… and that you never want to."…"

–Artie and Alice, Harper
