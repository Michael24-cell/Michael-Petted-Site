THE CLASS

Scene Study & On-Camera Training

Technique

Michael's class is based on moment-to-moment work — a real person in a real place — grounded in a behavior-oriented approach that uses imagination and lived experience to create authentic, compelling performances.

The training also emphasizes the realities of a working career, ensuring actors bridge the gap between training and professional work.

"Train with truth. Create with courage."

Primary Focus

The class primarily focuses on scene study, with students presenting fully rehearsed scenes that have been prepared outside of class with a partner.

Michael's class is a supportive, mixed-level environment where beginners, advanced actors, and working professionals can all thrive.

We also act on camera, do monologues and cold readings.

THE TEACHER

Michael Petted is one of Los Angeles' most dynamic and sought-after acting teachers. With over 15 years of experience, he has trained actors to break through creatively and professionally — helping them book roles, deepen their craft, and build lasting careers.

Michael has been a dedicated acting teacher since 2009, when he began teaching full-time at the renowned Beverly Hills Playhouse. There, he spent over a decade teaching and shaping the craft of hundreds of actors — working with many from the moment they began their training through to advanced levels of work. Rooted in the lineage of Milton Katselas, he taught actors through the foundational stages of discovery into deeper, more professional levels of training. This long-term developmental approach continues to inform his teaching today.

For eight years, Michael also served as the Head Acting Instructor for the Hollywood Immersive program, working with actors from around the world — including Australia, England, and Africa — teaching scene study and performance technique, challenging actors to achieve breakthroughs in their work while preparing them for the demands and realities of professional-level acting.

Today, Michael teaches his own class at The Hudson Theatre in Los Angeles, where his passion for growth, artistry, and practical technique continues to inspire actors at every level—from complete beginners to seasoned professionals.

The Playhouse is known for its celebrated alumni, including George Clooney, Michelle Pfeiffer, Alec Baldwin, Jorge Garcia, and Monica Barbaro.

Acting, directing, and music credits

Screen Credits

Student Spotlight

— Lance Miller

Actor & Filmmaker

I've had the privilege of knowing Michael Petted not only as an exceptional acting teacher, but as a mentor and dear friend. His guidance has shaped me profoundly, both as an artist and as a person.

Michael brings a distinctly directorial approach to his teaching. In his scene study class, actors rehearse and perform fully realized scenes with props, wardrobe, and blocking—creating an experience that feels much like working on a weekly play. This immersive, professional environment allows actors to engage with the work as it exists in real-world practice.

Like a director in rehearsal, Michael breaks down scenes with precision and insight, helping actors uncover specific beats and moments they can truly make their own. He explores multiple choices, challenges actors to go deeper, and then gives them the opportunity to immediately apply those discoveries through performance. This process trains actors to mine the script thoroughly and find the gold in every line.

Over the seven years I've worked with Michael, I've come into my own not only as an actor, but as a man. Through his mentorship, I've developed greater emotional intelligence, clearer and more honest self-expression, and the confidence to fully be myself—both in my work and in the world.

Michael's class is a supportive, mixed-level environment where beginners, advanced actors, and working professionals can all thrive. He has a rare ability to bring actors out of their shell while maintaining a safe space that encourages risk, growth, and authenticity. Actors seeking not only strong training, but also a collaborative community that regularly creates original projects—short films, feature films, and online media—will find his class especially rewarding.
