Terms of Use & Class Policy

Classes

Classes are offered in four-week sessions. Enrollment secures your place for the full four-week period.

Payment

Payment is collected in person and covers the full four-week session. This site does not process payments or store payment information.

All payments are non-refundable. Missed classes cannot be refunded or credited, as enrollment reserves a limited spot.

Students are expected to arrive on time and participate respectfully. Disruptive behavior may result in removal from class without refund.

Scheduling

Class schedules may change due to unforeseen circumstances. If a class must be canceled by the studio, reasonable efforts will be made to reschedule.

Use of Materials

Class content and materials are for personal use only and may not be recorded, reproduced, or shared without permission.
